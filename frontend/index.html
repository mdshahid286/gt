The process

From resume to explained verdict

Three stages, no single point of failure.

01

Four agents score independently

Skills, Experience, Education, and Fairness each evaluate the candidate on their own axis -- no agent sees another's opinion first.

02

Nash Bargaining decides

A hire only happens if every agent gains relative to its disagreement point -- one strong dissent is enough to fall back to reject.

03

Shapley value explains

Exact Shapley computation attributes the decision back to each agent's marginal contribution -- a real percentage, not a guess.

The panel

Meet the four agents

Each one focuses on a single axis and never sees the others' scores until Fairness reviews their reasoning.

Skills

Technical and domain skill match -- tools, languages, depth demonstrated through real projects, not keyword matching.

Experience

Relevant work history and trajectory -- never penalizes employment gaps by default.

Education

Relevance of academic background to the role -- bootcamps and self-taught paths score on merit, not prestige.

Fairness

Reviews the other three agents' reasoning for reliance on proxy signals -- never makes its own demographic guess.
